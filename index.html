1 · The Problem With a Comp

Sports cards don’t have a data shortage. They have a decision problem.

A sold price isn’t automatically useful evidence. It matters only when you understand what actually sold.

Data → Evidence → Decision

A comp has to earn the right to influence the decision.

Year, set, card number, parallel, variation, grader, grade, sale status, and market context can change what a price actually means. FlipForge is built to question that evidence before it becomes confidence.

4 · Grading Economics

The PSA 10 price isn’t your grading profit.

A raw card is not a PSA 10. Your return depends on what happens when the card comes back.

See the whole curve

Model the range before you submit.

FlipForge brings raw value, grading cost, multiple grade outcomes, expected value, break-even grade, and downside into the same evaluation.

Guessing the grade is guessing your return. The point is to understand what has to go right—and what happens when it doesn’t.
